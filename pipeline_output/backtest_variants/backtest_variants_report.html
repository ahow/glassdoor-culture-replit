Culture Quartile Backtest — Weight-Estimation Variants

Run date: 2026-07-27  |  Common start: 2015-12-31  |  Formation quarters: 43

This study compares three ways of setting the model weights used to rank companies in the quarterly culture-quartile backtest. Everything else (point-in-time review evidence, shrinkage, peer buckets, quartile formation, equal weighting, quarterly rebalancing, prices, benchmark) is identical across variants, so differences are attributable to the weights alone.

  • Variant C — Current: today's production weights applied at all snapshots (the existing dashboard method).
  • Variant A — Averaged: a fixed weight vector per peer bucket, the confidence-weighted average of ridge fits from all 12 rolling 5-year windows (2015 through mid-2026).
  • Variant W — Walk-forward: at each snapshot, weights averaged only over windows completed by that date. Never uses future information — the honest variant.
Bottom line: Variant C's +6.7pp/yr Q1−Q4 spread splits into −1.0pp (2016–2020) vs +13.4pp (2021–present) — concentrated in the years its weights were fitted on. The honest walk-forward variant (W) shows +7.9pp before 2021 but −0.9pp since 2021. Much of the current headline spread therefore reflects look-ahead in the weights, not a signal that was usable at the time. The measurement framework — not the fitted weighting — is the defensible claim.

8. Caveats

Variant A look-ahead: Variant A is NOT free of look-ahead: every evaluation quarter lies inside at least one estimation window that fed the average. It is a robustness comparison, not the honest benchmark; Variant W is the walk-forward (honest) variant.
  • 69 window×bucket cells were flagged thin (estimation set under 60% of today's size), mostly 2015–2017 — early-window results rest on fewer companies.
  • Survivorship: universe is today's company list; the same look-ahead/survivorship caveats as the published backtest apply to prices and membership.
  • Returns are local-currency, price-only, equal-weighted, no transaction costs.
  • Confidence weighting: sum over estimation set of company total scored reviews as of window end. Turnover: one-way: |names added to Q1| / |Q1|, averaged over rebalances.
